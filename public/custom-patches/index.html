Qualification policy

We define “qualified” before we launch.

Your sales team should know exactly what it is paying for.

Criteria are agreed with you in writing before a single campaign goes live — so both sides are working to the same definition.

  1. 01

    Patch category

    Only approved products count.

    • Embroidered
    • PVC
    • Chenille
    • Woven
    • Leather
    • Printed

    The RFQ can require:

    • Quantity
    • Patch Type
    • Size
    • Backing
    • Deadline
    • Project Details
  4. 04

    Artwork

    Artwork, logo or reference-file requirements can be defined according to your sales process.

  5. 05

    • Buyer Type

    • Other criteria
