## 2. How the timing was measured

- **Phrase boundaries:** silence detection on the recording found 19 spoken phrases, the same number of lines as the transcript (the script fails if they ever disagree).
- **Word timing:** pocketsphinx forced alignment (offline) of each phrase against its known words succeeded for all 19 phrases (`src/ad03/vo-timing.json`, `method: forced-alignment`).
- **Transcript:** obtained by speech recognition plus per-phrase candidate scoring, because online models were blocked by the network. Two short lines were the least certain and should be confirmed by ear:
  - 10.54 s: "We'll create one for you." Screen copy uses the styleframe line "WE CAN CREATE IT FOR YOU." (same meaning).
  - 12.09 s: "Get free artwork support," which scored above "free design support".
- **VO placement:** starts at frame 3 (0.1 s), immediately after the opening impact, and plays complete and unaltered to 35.43 s. The end card holds 2.07 s after the last word.

## 5. Sound

`scripts/sound-design-ad03.py`, mixed by `./scripts/build-audio.sh ad03`. Stems: `public/audio/ad03-stem-{vo,bed}.wav`.

- **Voiceover on top.** Light high-pass and ±4 dB levelling. The music is side-chain ducked about 14 dB and the effects about 9 dB under it. Measured voice-over-bed while speaking: median **18.5 dB**, 10th percentile 10.2 dB.
- **Music: TEMP, original.** A 100 BPM bed (kick, clap, hats, 808, electric keys, pads) synthesized in this repo. It builds from 25 s and resolves on a G chord with a 0.9 s ring-out.
- **Effects (all synthesized, no samples):** opening impact; glove and fabric handling; embroidery-machine needle clatter under the machine shots (quiet under the voice); thread zip on the stitched reveals; pencil scratch on the sketch; impact on "No problem!"; low hit on "$0"; whooshes on the match cut, wipe and whip; ticks on the word montage; logo bells and sparkle; CTA accent; final resolve.
- **Packaging sounds** were left out because no packaging appears on screen.

## 6. Unresolved warnings (client sign-off before publishing)

1. **Music is a temporary original bed, not a licensed track.** Replace it with a licensed instrumental (any tempo; the effects stem stays in sync with picture) before launch.
2. **Claims spoken and shown that do not appear in the Brand Identity System:** $0 / zero setup fees; unlimited artwork revisions; USA-based business; US-to-US shipping; free backing and shipping.
3. **Comparative pricing claim:** the VO says "some of the best prices on custom patches". The screen shows the softer **COMPETITIVE PRICES**. Comparative claims need substantiation.
4. **Transcript confidence:** confirm lines 7 and 8 (see §2) by ear.
5. **Missing footage:** the 4th clip was not supplied (duplicate upload). No apparel-application footage exists, so "hats / jackets / uniforms" is carried by typography over real stitching footage, not invented product shots.
6. **Styleframe 04's warehouse / shipping environment was not recreated,** because it would read as documentary proof of operations that we have no footage of.

## 7. Technical notes

- **Shot conforming:** every shot is conformed by FFmpeg from `src/ad03/edl.json` (`scripts/conform-shots.py ad03`) to an exact-length 1080×1920 / 30 fps segment. That means trim, retime, motion-compensated 24→30 fps for slow moves, Lanczos upscale with light sharpening, and the `look: "sketch"` pencil rendering.
- **No stretching:** footage is never stretched. The framing uses uniform scale and offset only.
- **After Effects:** not used. All motion design is Remotion (React/TypeScript): springs, interpolation, SVG masks, directional SVG motion blur, CSS compositing.
- **Final QC:** both files fully decoded and checked:
  - 1125 frames; no black frames; aspect ratio 9:16
  - audio stream complete with no clipping
  - representative frames reviewed from the rendered output (`out/qa/`)
